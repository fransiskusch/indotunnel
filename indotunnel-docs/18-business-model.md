# Business Model

## 1. Strategy

Launch Free first.

The early objective is product validation and usage data rather than immediate revenue.

Capture metrics such as:

- signups
- activated users
- active tunnels
- requests/day
- GB/day
- tunnel duration
- retention
- webhook usage

## 4. Cost Drivers

The largest technical cost driver is usually network bandwidth, followed by persistent compute/connections and storage/observability.

A low user count can still produce high cost if users proxy large files or long-lived streams.

## 5. Monetization Trigger

Do not add payment just because the product works.

Add paid plans when there is evidence of users wanting:

- persistent tunnels
- custom domains
- higher bandwidth
- higher quotas
- team collaboration
- advanced access controls

## 6. Potential Indonesian Positioning

Differentiation can focus on:

- simple Indonesian-friendly onboarding
- local developer community
- webhook workflows commonly used in Indonesian payments and integrations
- local support/content
- affordable paid plans
